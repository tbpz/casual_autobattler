## The synthesis

Underlying shape across all of them: **a simple input produces a big, visible, real-feeling consequence.** Splits into two poles by who gets credit:

- **Pole A — "I'm clever / I earned this."** (Hades, TFT, Diablo) Payoff = pride, credit = me. Engine: building/gambling.
- **Pole B — "the world is alive."** (WoW, Zelda) Payoff = wonder, credit = the world reacting. Engine: watching/spectacle.

**Key live-data point — Draft Showdown:** cracked the "rush archers/assassins" solve fast (Pole A fired), then went stale — *"little strategic choice, that's all I can think of."* Started craving "surprise interaction or synergy between units" (Pole B). This showed the dependency: **Pole A is the entry but decays once solved; Pole B (surprising, combinatorial synergy) is what refills Pole A** — every emergent interaction is new material to be clever about. Shallow synergy → decay → boredom (confirmed independently in Draft Showdown, the prototype, and the original flat build — three separate signals of the same failure).

This is trustworthy evidence because Draft Showdown is a game I only *played*, not designed — unlike prototype-boredom, which is suspect self-evidence (STATE's own caution: a moment's designer already knows the solve, so their own boredom isn't reliable evidence for it).

## The crystallized lead moment (candidate — mine only, not yet shared)

> **"I assemble a squad, press play, and watch my synergy run away past what I planned — a chain reaction cascading across the battlefield, bigger and stranger than I expected, yet unmistakably mine to have built."**

Load-bearing clauses (each one is a test a mechanic must pass):
- *"assemble … press play … watch"* → watch-native, not fighting watch-only combat.
- *"run away past what I planned"* → Balatro-style refill; demands synergy deep enough to surprise its own author. Rejects thin/shallow drafts.
- *"chain reaction cascading across the battlefield"* → Zelda-style reactivity; demands a living board. Rejects variance-off, dead terrain.
- *"unmistakably mine to have built"* → keeps payoff attributable; rejects illegible chaos / coin-flip outcomes.

**Escape-hatch bar for self-judgment:** since I designed the game, I can't trust my own "surprise" the normal way — the target is a synergy system combinatorial enough that even *I* (the author) still get surprised by it, the way Balatro's own dev does.

Alternate phrasings (same 4 clauses, different lead pole):
- Cleverness-forward: *"The moment a combo I engineered detonates far bigger than I intended — and I know it ran that far because of what I chose."*
- Wonder-forward: *"The moment I light one spark and the whole board goes off in a cascade I set up but couldn't fully predict."*
