Bets

Last updated: 2026-10-28

A bet is a choice a good game could reasonably make the other way. A tuning target is a goal every good game must hit. 2026-10-28

Every line serves one pillar. A line that serves none widens the game, it does not deepen it.

A decision that breaks a verified line is flagged to Tu before it is logged, never applied silently.

Pillars

  • A. The fight moment — "It blew up bigger than I expected, and I made that happen."
  • B. The run — "My choices in this run mattered."
  • C. Coming back — "One more run; the next one will be different, and I keep getting better."
  • D. Pick up and play — "I got it in minutes on my phone, and a run fits my sitting."

Verified — the quality standard

A. The fight moment

  • The lead moment lands in plain runs. 2026-10-23
  • Rune synergy is the strongest source of "mine"; chain luck is the lesser. 2026-10-23
  • Runes must combine with what the squad's skills do. 2026-10-23
  • Chains run unexpectedly long both ways; payoff and backfire both surprise. 2026-08-17
  • A player can name what caused a chain. 2026-08-25
  • The big payoff is rare; one every fight feels scripted. 2026-08-06
  • Spectacle plays only when the payoff is real. 2026-08-06
  • Every HP loss has a visible actor and cause. 2026-08-04
  • RNG alone fails "claim as mine". 2026-07-26

B. The run

  • The best squad can still lose. 2026-08-08
  • A run grows as it goes; it never only shrinks. 2026-09-23
  • A run never ends on something the player could not change. 2026-09-23
  • A real decision brings new options, never leaves the default better, never collapses to one move. 2026-07-26
  • Watching with no real choice reads as an idle game. 2026-10-17
  • A re-skin alone gives an army no identity. 2026-10-21
  • An army's own runes must act on its own mechanic; a rune that works with the army taken out gives no identity. 2026-10-23

C. Coming back

  • Chance alone stops holding Tu once the best move is known. 2026-07-24
  • A solved deterministic build stays solved. 2026-07-22

D. Pick up and play

  • A term is explained where the player first meets it. 2026-10-10

Bets — test in this order

Click a line to see its questions; answer them once after the run, never during it. Tune a bet only until its test is fair, then play it once. Drop it, and go the other way, if it fails a fair test, cannot reach one in 3 tries, or what it rests on goes away. A bug, something you could not see, or "didn't come up" does not count: play again.

  1. BPush or rest stays a real choice; tired units get benched by hand.

    Fair test when: a run puts a fielded unit near its backfire point while a fresh one waits on the bench.

    Play: one full run.

    • Did I bench a tired unit myself at least once?
    • Was there a moment I wasn't sure whether to push or rest?

    Pass: yes to both.

  2. BAn army gives a run an identity worth picking.

    Fair test when: the Mage synergy tuning target has passed, and each Mage build shows on screen.

    Play: 2 Mages runs with different builds (Vessel, Fuse or Kernel).

    • Did I pick runes differently because of my build?
    • Did I watch something different in the fights? (a few words)
    • Next time at the army pick, do I have a reason to pick this one?

    Pass: yes to the first and the last.

  3. DA run fits a short sitting.

    Needs a new player; outside tests are paused.

    Fair test when: one run on the phone takes a few minutes (time one yourself).

    Play: an outside player, on their own phone, whenever they like.

    • When did they reach for it? (in a short gap, or settled in)
    • Did they want the run longer, shorter, or as it was?
    • Did they stop before the end?

    Pass: played in short gaps, with no ask for longer runs.

  4. DA new player wins about 1 run in 4.

    Needs a new player.

    Fair test when: the bot's run completion for the plain game is near 1 in 4 (npm run batch).

    Play: their first 8 or more runs.

    • How many runs, how many wins?
    • After a loss, did they start another right away?
    • Did a loss feel fair or cheap? (a few words)

    Pass: about 1 in 4, and losses led to another run.

Tuning targets — in work order

Never dropped: tune until the Pass line holds. 3 played tries; out of tries, look again at the bets under it. Tuning that makes a bet's test fair comes just before that bet; tuning under an unverified bet waits.

  1. AMage runes and marks bring the synergy moment to Mage runs.

    Makes bet 2 a fair test.

    Play: 2 Mages runs (?army=mages).

    • Did two of my runes fire together for a big moment? (which round)
    • Did it feel like I built it, not luck?

    Pass: yes to both, in at least one run.

    Tries: 1 of 3. 2026-10-28: no big moment, the build was collected, not chosen, and most runes did not show. 2026-10-28

  2. AThe same works for the Undead and Beasts.

    Waits on bet 2; their runes are not built.

    Play: 1 Undead run, 1 Beasts run.

    • Did two of my runes fire together for a big moment? (which round, per army)
    • Did it feel like I built it, not luck? (per army)

    Pass: yes to both, for each army.

  3. DSkeletons, the pounce and orbs stay readable on a phone.

    Play: one fight per army, on a real phone.

    • Could I tell what my army did without pausing? (per army)
    • Was there anything I couldn't see or had to squint at? (name it)

    Pass: yes for every army, nothing named. This tests seeing, not understanding; you cannot unknow the rules.

  4. DA first-time player reads the game unprompted.

    Needs a new player; outside tests are paused.

    Play: watch them, don't explain.

    • What did they ask about? (list)
    • Did they get stuck on any screen? (which)
    • After the run, could they say why they won or lost?

    Pass: no stuck screen, and they can say why.

  5. CA player starts another run right after one ends, win or lose.

    Play: just play; you count as the player for now.

    • After a win, did I start another right away?
    • After a loss, did I start another right away?

    Pass: yes to both, across a few sessions.

  6. CRun 20 still shows something new.

    The 20 is a bet of its own.

    Play: just play; keep one line per run wherever you like.

    • Did I see something new this run? (one line)

    Pass: still yes around run 20.

  7. TopSomeone not on the team finishes a run and starts another without being asked.

    The top test: what every tuning target adds up to. Needs a new player.

    Play: hand them the phone, then say nothing.

    • Did they finish the run?
    • Did they start another on their own?

    Pass: yes to both.
