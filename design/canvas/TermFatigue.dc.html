Fatigue
Every fight tires a unit, and losing HP or backfiring tires it more. A tired unit's skill is up to 35% stronger and its chain runs longer, but it backfires more often, and a backfire does the opposite of what the skill is for. A unit on the bench loses 20 fatigue each round. Watch its eyes: the lids droop and the whites redden as it tires.
Closes on ✕, Got it, or a tap outside the card. Words inside a card are coloured but not tappable; closing always returns to the screen. Frame 1.1 is only a stand-in behind it: fatigue does not appear on that screen.
