Marks and guard pips sit on the body, so the field never jumps
Today a unit's Exposed / Burn badges (and a guarding tank's pips) are rows inside its card. The row is not there until the mark lands, so the card grows, and everything below it is shoved down. The fix, chosen 2026-10-02 and built, draws both on the body instead, out of the layout. Each picture below is a pair: nothing held, then the same marks landed. The dashed amber lines mark where the centre line and the callout band were in the left picture. Green means that line did not move; red means it did.

A · On the body — chosen, built
Badges are a strip drawn over the bottom edge of the body, out of the layout like the freeze ring. Guard pips sit in a dark pill in the upper part of the body (the freeze timer's pill style, placed above it so a frozen guarding tank still shows its countdown). No row exists, so nothing can grow.
What it costs. The strip covers a slice of the chain ring at the 6 o'clock position and the lower edge of small bodies; two big stacks on a 24px body hide most of it (see edge cases).
