A · a skill fires and repeats
The bubble sits on Ivy, over her head: EXPOSE ×3, one fixed size. The skill is in her role colour, the count is yellow, and the yellow border is closed all round it, arrow included. She glows and the rest of the squad is dimmed, name, HP and bar too; a violet line runs to the Vanguard and the third hit is orange (−29). Her charge bar is empty while the skill plays (it drained into the bubble when it fired, see card 3.9); Sol's bar is full, steady yellow and dimmed, because she is ready and waits for Ivy's skill to end. A card fires on the start (yellow popup with its cause). Hit numbers are card 3.9's (18, 23, 29, 34).

The bubble keeps its size, and says what is going on
Every skill use is announced on the unit that did it, in one bubble of one size: the skill alone, then the count beside it from the second hit, a brighter glow from the fifth, MAX at the cap, STOPPED when it ends (grey, red after a backfire), and BACKFIRE in red while a backfire runs. The bubble never grows, so a long run shows in the count and the glow. The word "chain" is not used in the fight; it stays on the cards and popups.
