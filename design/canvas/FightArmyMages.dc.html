D · Mages: orbs that merge and strike
Skill hits add orbs, three at most. Every 2 seconds each orb works alone: Ivy's throw lightning at one enemy, Bram's shield him, Sol's heal him. A 4th orb pulls them all into one sparkling orb that strikes at once (Frost shields the team, Life heals it) and grows with every hit after. When the skill ends, the orbs are gone.
