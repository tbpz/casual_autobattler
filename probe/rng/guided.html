RNG Probe — guided walkthrough

A handful of short stages, each adds one new soldier. Every soldier rolls its own dice, independently — nobody reacts to anybody else's roll, there's no chain to engineer. Your only job is to pick which gambles to take; the dice do the rest. Press Play a few times per stage and watch the score jump around — the same row gives a genuinely different number every roll.
How to read a roll: ■ hit ■ crit ■ jackpot ■ miss — nothing ever wakes up another soldier.
